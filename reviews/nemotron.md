- **Prestige**: 1 run completed with permanent multipliers (gold ×1.1,
  production ×1.05)

## Active Quests (All First Step Completed)

1. **"Первые дрова"** — 119/20 wood collected, need to craft axe
2. **"Каменный век"** — 54/25 stone collected, need to craft hammer (requires
   crafting lvl 3)
3. **"Первый трофей"** — 3/3 wolves killed, need to craft hide (requires hunting
   lvl 9)

## What Was Interesting

- **Quest dependency chain**: gather → craft → complete step. Well-structured
  progression.
- **Upgrade system**: axes and pickaxes give multiplicative production bonuses.
- **Prestige system**: resetting progress for permanent multipliers.
- **Different zones**: meadow (rabbits, wolves), forest (boars), quarry, swamp —
  each with unique monsters and loot.
- **Market**: buy/sell prices, trading for gold.
- **Bank**: deposits with interest and automatic debt repayment.

## Issues / Bugs / UX Concerns

- **Hunting doesn't give XP from monster kills** — wolves and rabbits don't
  increase hunting level, unclear XP mechanic.
- **Only one action at a time** — can't gather and craft simultaneously.
- **Some recipes locked behind high skill levels** — iron ore requires mining
  lvl 8, currently at 5.
- **Passive income mechanics unclear** — resources have ratePerMinute but
  implementation opaque.
- **Crafting requires high skill levels** — crafting lvl 3 needed for hammer,
  progression slow.

## Final Verdict

MCP Idle Game is a solid idle RPG with deep crafting, progressive quests, and
interesting mechanics (prestige, upgrades, marketplace).

**Strengths**: Well-defined task chain, clear progression loop, meaningful
upgrades, variety of resources and zones.

**Areas for improvement**: Clarity on skill XP gain (especially hunting),
multi-action capability, early-game resource balance for crafting.

For fans of idle games with RPG and crafting elements — there's plenty to engage
with. Has a learning curve but the core loop is very engaging.
